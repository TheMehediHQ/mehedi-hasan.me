## Persuasion & Influence

A deep dive into the psychology of influence, persuasion, and human behavior.

1. **Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion** — Robert B. Cialdini
2. **Pre-Suasion** — Robert B. Cialdini
3. **Yes!: 50 Scientifically Proven Ways to Be Persuasive** — Noah J. Goldstein, Steve J. Martin, Robert B. Cialdini
4. **How to Win Friends and Influence People** — Dale Carnegie
